Chapter 1 · Weight arithmetic — the first family

Subtract the direction of a fact,
and watch it drain away.

A tiny language model learned the capitals of the world. One request comes in: forget that the capital of Ukraine is Kyiv. Task-vector negation tries to do it with pure arithmetic on the weights — no retraining. Everything below is a live forward pass of the real model running in your browser.

1 · Four models, four jobs

Unlearning juggles a small cast of models — all branching from the same base θ₀, each just θ₀ tuned on a different slice of data. The one we actually want to fix is θ_full; the others exist only to help.

θ₀base
— tuned on all capitals →
θ_full the model we must fix · knows Kyiv
θ_fulldeployed
— briefly tuned further on Kyiv alone →
θ_reinf the fact drilled deeper — a measuring stick for the "Kyiv direction"
θ₀base
— tuned on all capitals except Kyiv →
θ₋f the ideal — correct, but expensive to retrain

The whole trick: rather than pay to retrain θ₋f, we push the deployed model to be more sure of Kyiv for a few steps, measure that direction v_f = θ_reinf − θ_full, and then walk the opposite way. Can cheap arithmetic land near the expensive ideal? That is exactly what the next panel tests.

θ(α) = θ_full − α · v_f
α = 0.00

Drag α. It drives the weight-space vectors, the activations flowing through the model, and the output — all recomputed live.

2 · Measure → negate → apply, in weight space

The whole model is one point in weight space. Here it is projected to 2D: the horizontal axis is the "learn every capital" direction, the vertical axis is the "reinforce Kyiv" direction we measured. Negation slides θ_full backwards along −v_f.

7 · What this establishes — and what it does not

What it establishes

  • Exact arithmetic on a measured fine-tuning direction lowers the target answer's probability.
  • Lowering one answer necessarily raises others — probability is conserved, so Kyiv's mass flows to other capitals.
  • Forget and retain are entangled: past a point, removing Kyiv damages capitals we meant to keep.

What it does not

  • This is an approximate inverse of learning, not exact deletion — notice θ(α) never lands on θ₋f.
  • A colored square is one activation or a module norm — not the location where a fact is stored.
  • A suppressed next token is not proof the knowledge is gone (Wu et al. 2025 recover "unlearned" facts).
  • The construction matters: building v_f from a θ₀-branch (instead of reinforcing at θ_full) behaves non-monotonically on this very model — task vectors are configuration-sensitive (Dong et al. 2025).
