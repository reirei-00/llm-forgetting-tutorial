Chapter 3 · Representation steering — the third family

Don't rewrite the weights.
Move the thought.

Chapters 1–2 attacked the weights. This family attacks the activations: the model's internal state as it thinks. It already has a region that means "I can't answer" — the mythical lands it never learned. Steering pushes the Ukraine-state into that region. Every number below is a live forward pass with a real injected vector.

1 · The residual stream, and a direction in it

Inside the transformer, every token carries a running 32-dim vector — the residual stream — that each block reads and edits. At the last token ("is"), that vector is the model's current thought about the answer. We compute one direction from real activations:

r = mean h(mythical lands) − h(ukraine)

— the straight line from "the thought that answers Kyiv" to "the thought that answers unknown". At inference we simply add it: h ← h + β·r. No loss, no training, no weight change.

5 · What this establishes — and what it does not

What it establishes

  • A single measured direction in activation space redirects the answer to a coherent "unknown" — no training at all.
  • The trade-off is visible: blanket steering costs retained accuracy; the baked (LUNAR-style) edit uses retain data to avoid that.
  • Interventions on representations are a genuinely different family from weight arithmetic and optimization.

What it does not

  • Slide β back to 0. Kyiv returns, exactly. The knowledge never left the weights — steering is a mask, and this chapter proves it interactively.
  • The baked version changes one matrix — but activation-space attacks can still recover "unlearned" content (Wu et al. 2025; steering is itself used to extract unlearned facts, Seyitoğlu et al. 2024).
  • A coherent refusal is controllable behaviour, not absence of knowledge.
